minen conjugation -> ich mine, du minst, es mint (not "minet"/"minest"); noun forms Mining, Miner
numbers in prose (coordinator rule, translation-guide rule 3) -> German separators: 6.000 BNT, 2.097.152, 0,1 BNT, 49,4 H/s; code, inline code, commands, quoted program output and key=value literals (amount=0.5) stay byte-identical; years, versions (v0.2.16, glibc 2.39, Argon2id version 1.3) and model numbers get no separator; percent with a space: 56 %
